# Mandarin risk register

Baseline: 2026-10-18. All risks are open until evidence resolves them. Owners below are roles to assign when staffing is known.

| ID | Severity | Risk / consequence | Owner and resolving milestone | Required experiment or mitigation | Stop condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| R-002 | Critical | Worker isolation fails on stock Ubuntu due to AppArmor/user-namespace policy or required office privileges | Systems engineer, M0 S0 | Packaged policy and scoped worker on clean 24.04/26.04; network/FS escape fixtures; require no host-wide policy changes | No unconfined fallback in certified paths; redesign packaging/confinement |

| R-004 | High | PDF → DOCX/PPTX/XLSX/EPUB output opens but is visually or semantically unusable | Document engineer, M0 P0/M4 | Digital/scanned/multicolumn/table corpora; independent editability and reading-order review | A flat raster wrapper cannot pass editable reconstruction; explicit scope/fidelity decision required |

The primary global-drag experiment, confined-worker experiment, Pages writer, PDF reconstruction and offline dependency closure are scheduled before feature expansion. Unknown feasibility is not equivalent to an accepted exclusion. Track results and decisions in [SOP](../../SOP.md) evidence records and the architecture's open questions.
